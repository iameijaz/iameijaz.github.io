MK 13 · Competition · Team NUST-AG (Pakistan) · Indonesia

Driver-assistance unit for Team NUST-AG (Pakistan) at Shell Eco-marathon 2024 on the Mandalika Circuit, Indonesia. A Raspberry Pi 4 combines GPS with on-board sensors (gyroscope, IMU) to locate the car on the circuit and tells the driver to pulse or glide for maximum mileage. The pulse/glide points come from a lap simulation of Mandalika. The driver display shows fuel, acceleration, live estimated mileage and the optimal gear. Pit-to-driver messages and remote telemetry (mileage, health metrics, temperature) run over LoRa to a laptop. Built in several variants.
